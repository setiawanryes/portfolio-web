Course Scheduling

Goal Programming Optimization at ITERA

I began working on this thesis because I previously had experience analyzing scheduling issues at BPS East Lampung during the Covid-19 period. From that project, I saw firsthand how manual scheduling could create many problems like overlaps and inefficient workload allocation.

As a student in the Mathematics Study Program at ITERA, I noticed that semester schedules were often not optimal—conflicting class times, overloaded days, and mismatches with lecturers’ preferences were common issues.

Realizing this is an optimization problem, I applied Goal Programming. This method is highly suitable for handling multiple constraints (hard & soft constraints) simultaneously to satisfy institutional rules and preferences.

Through this research, I formulated a mathematical model to produce an optimal even-semester course schedule that avoids conflicts and provides convenience for both students and lecturers.
