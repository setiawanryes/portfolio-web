The Goal Programming Model for Employee Scheduling at BPS East Lampung During the Covid-19 Pandemic

During my internship, I noticed that the employee schedule at BPS East Lampung was still arranged manually and did not fully comply with the Covid-19 health protocols. Seeing this issue, I took the initiative to apply my background as a mathematics student to develop a more optimal scheduling model. I aimed to create a system that could support both work from office (WFO) and work from home (WFH) arrangements according to government regulations during the pandemic.

Employee scheduling became an essential aspect after Indonesia confirmed its first Covid-19 case on March 2, 2020. The government introduced several measures such as social distancing, physical distancing, and large-scale social restrictions (PSBB). In response, BPS East Lampung—an institution responsible for providing official data—had to adjust its workflow following Circular Letter No. 19/2020 regarding the new work system for civil servants.

The policy intensified on June 4, 2020, through a new circular issued by the Governor of Lampung, emphasizing the transition toward a productive and safe new normal. The regulation required government offices to manage alternating WFO and WFH schedules, which made a structured and compliant scheduling system even more crucial.

In mathematics, scheduling problems can be effectively solved using the Goal Programming model. This method is a variation of linear programming that handles multiple goals simultaneously by minimizing deviations based on priority levels. Goal Programming has been widely applied in various fields such as production planning, cost minimization, and healthcare scheduling.

Therefore, in this internship project, I designed a mathematical formulation using Goal Programming to optimize the employee scheduling system at BPS East Lampung. The resulting schedule aims to satisfy government regulations, meet operational needs, and create an efficient arrangement during the Covid-19 pandemic.
