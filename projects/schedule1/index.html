Applying Goal Programming for Course Scheduling Optimization: A Case Study in the Mathematics Study Program at ITERA

I began working on this thesis because I previously had experience analyzing scheduling issues at BPS East Lampung during the Covid-19 period. From that project, I saw firsthand how manual scheduling could create many problems when it was not optimized—overlaps, conflicts, and inefficient workload allocation. That experience stayed with me, and it made me more aware when I noticed similar issues happening in my own academic environment.

As a student in the Mathematics Study Program at ITERA, I often experienced that the course schedules published each semester were still not optimal. Conflicting class times, overloaded days, limited rooms, and mismatches with lecturers’ preferences were problems that many students—including myself—faced every semester. With the limited number of lecturers and classrooms, creating a manual schedule became increasingly difficult, and the results often did not fully satisfy either lecturers or students.

From these observations, I realized that this problem could actually be approached using a structured mathematical method. Scheduling is fundamentally an optimization problem, and methods such as Goal Programming are highly suitable for handling multiple constraints and preferences simultaneously. Previous studies have shown that this method can produce practical, fast, and optimal scheduling results while fulfilling all hard constraints and minimizing soft constraint violations.

With this understanding, I felt motivated to use my background as a mathematics student to design a better scheduling model. Through this research, I aim to produce an optimal even-semester course schedule for the Mathematics Study Program at ITERA—one that avoids conflicts, satisfies institutional constraints, and provides convenience for both students and lecturers.

In other words, this thesis was inspired by a combination of real-world experience, actual needs within my department, and the desire to contribute a more effective solution for course scheduling in the Mathematics Study Program at ITERA.

🧩 Key Features of Course Scheduling Optimization

  • 📚
    Optimal Course Allocation: Assigning courses to the most suitable time slots and rooms.
  • 👩‍🏫
    Lecturer Availability Matching: Ensuring schedules align with lecturer availability and preferences.
  • ⚖️
    Balanced Workload: Distributing teaching loads fairly across lecturers and semesters.
  • 🚫
    Conflict Minimization: Avoiding schedule overlaps between courses, lecturers, and student groups.
  • 🔄
    Flexible Adjustment: Easily adaptable when there are curriculum or lecturer changes.
  • 📈
    Efficiency Improvement: Enhancing the overall effectiveness of course planning.
