SI-TANDUR

Integrated Agricultural Information System

About The Project

SI-TANDUR is an Integrated Agricultural Information System developed specifically for East Lampung to help local farmers plan, monitor, and manage the planting process more efficiently. It provides crop planning, real-time monitoring, and data-based care and harvest recommendations.

I developed this project as part of the 2025 East Lampung Regional Innovation and Technology Competition. The goal is to modernize the agricultural sector through digital transformation.

SI-TANDUR is designed as a digital platform for both web and mobile, integrating:

  • Agricultural information & Production data
  • Market prices & Weather information
  • Promotional hub for MITRA & MSMEs

This system serves as a modern solution supporting agriculture and MSME development in the digital era.
