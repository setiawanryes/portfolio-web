LevelUp Arcade

Interactive Educational Math Game Platform

LevelUp Arcade is an educational platform designed to enhance mathematical skills through immersive and gamified experiences. The project aims to transform traditional learning into a series of engaging challenges that foster a love for numbers.

The platform features a variety of game mechanics—from multiplayer puzzles to real-time battles—specifically engineered to train critical thinking, memory retention, and rapid-fire mental arithmetic.

By combining entertainment with pedagogy, LevelUp Arcade provides a comprehensive approach for users to sharpen their logic and calculation speed in a competitive yet fun environment.

Math Cross

A multiplayer numerical crossword. Solve arithmetic puzzles (+, -, *, /) to sharpen logic and memory.

Maze Goat

Strategic multiplayer adventure. Guide a lost sheep through a forest by solving math problems within 10 seconds.

Math Verse

High-speed grid challenge. Perform calculations and click the correct grid within a 1-minute time limit.

Math Pop Battle

Competitive PvP mode. Answer rapidly to inflate and pop your opponent's balloon before they pop yours!
